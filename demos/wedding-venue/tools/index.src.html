04Your day

From first look to last dance.

1.30 pm

  1. 1.30 pm

    Vows in the walled garden

    Your guests gather on the lawn and walk through the old door in the wall. Twenty-five minutes, a string trio, and a shower of dried delphinium petals on the way out.

  2. 3.00 pm

    Drinks by the lake

    Canapés, English fizz and lawn games. Your photographer takes you down to the jetty for twenty minutes of golden light while nobody is looking.

  3. 5.30 pm

    The wedding breakfast

    Your reception dinner: long tables, three courses from our kitchen garden, and speeches as the light softens through the great doors.

  4. 9.30 pm

    Into the night

    The band starts, the festoon comes on in the oaks, and the late licence runs to midnight. Sparkler exit on the terrace, then a nightcap in the library.

  5. Sunday, 10.00 am

    Brunch in the house

    Bacon rolls, strong coffee and the story of last night told twelve different ways. Checkout is at eleven, and nobody wants to leave.

09Good questions

Asked by every couple.

Something else? Ask Charlotte, she replies within four working hours.

Can we marry legally at Wrenfold?

Yes. The walled garden, tithe barn, orangery and lake lawn are all licensed for civil ceremonies, and we welcome your own celebrant or officiant and religious ceremonies. Couples from abroad often marry legally at home and celebrate here; Charlotte will walk you through giving notice in England if you'd like the legal ceremony here too.

What does “exclusive use” mean?

The whole estate is yours and nobody else's: no other wedding, no hotel guests, no public. From noon on the day you arrive until 11 am on the day you leave, every room, garden and gate is for you and your guests.

Do you hold dates?

Yes, free of charge for seven days after you choose one online or at a show-round. A 25% deposit confirms the date, and the balance is due eight weeks before the wedding.

Can we bring our own caterer?

Our kitchen cooks for every wedding, using the walled garden and local farms. For cultural menus we partner with specialist caterers we trust, and we're happy to talk about yours.

What happens if it rains?

Every plan has a wet-weather twin: ceremonies move to the orangery or barn, drinks to the barn terrace under cover. We decide together at 10 am on the day.

Is there a curfew or noise limit?

Live music runs until 11.30 pm and the bar until midnight. After that, the house is yours, quietly.

Is the estate accessible?

The barn, orangery, lawn and two ground-floor bedrooms have step-free access, and we have a golf buggy for the walled garden path.

Can we bring our dog?

Of course. Dogs are welcome at the ceremony and in the cottages; we'll even find them a minder for the evening.
